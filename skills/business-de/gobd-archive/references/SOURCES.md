## Conflicts / open questions
- The 10-year vs 6-year split: 10 years applies to Grundstücke/Immobilien and Wertpapiere mit Verwahrung; everything else 6 years. Re-verify against HGB §257 if a future amendment changes this.
- GoBD 2021 does not yet mandate a specific archive format; the "unveränderliche Ablage" requirement is procedural, not format-bound. Do not claim format mandates the law does not state.
