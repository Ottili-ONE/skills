## Corrections under sign-off

1. **§13b reverse charge is NOT "services only".** The canonical skill states it is.
   §13b ("Leistungsempfänger als Steuerschuldner") shifts the VAT debt to the recipient
   for a *defined list of goods AND services* (Abs. 1 Nr. 1–11 UStG, incl. Werklieferungen,
   Bau-/Renovierungsleistungen an Immobilien, certain intra-community goods acquisitions,
   IT/consulting/construction services). The goods-vs-services split used by the canonical
   skill is an oversimplification; the full Abs. 1 list must be consulted per transaction.
2. **Intra-community delivery (igL) is NOT §4a UStG.** igL = **UStG §4 Nr. 1b** with the
   substantive rule in **UStG §6a** (steuerfreie innergemeinschaftliche Lieferung). §4a
   UStG is "Steuervergütung" (tax refund) and is not the igL citation.
